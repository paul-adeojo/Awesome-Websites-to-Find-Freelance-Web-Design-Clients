# Awesome-Websites-to-Find-Freelance-Web-Design-Clients
A curated list of websites and platforms where freelance designers and developers can find potential clients, freelance projects, and web design opportunities.

This list focuses on platforms useful for web designers, UI/UX designers, Framer designers, Webflow developers, React developers, and other digital professionals.

## Platforms for Finding Clients

### ThemesMotion

https://themesmotion.com/

ThemesMotion helps designers and developers showcase their expertise and portfolio while connecting with potential founders and businesses looking for freelance design and development services.

### Upwork

https://www.upwork.com/

A large freelance marketplace covering web design, development, UI/UX, marketing, writing, and many other professional services.

### Contra

https://contra.com/

A platform for independent professionals to showcase their work, find opportunities, and manage freelance work.

### Fiverr

https://www.fiverr.com/

A freelance services platform where designers and developers can offer specific services to clients.

### Toptal

https://www.toptal.com/

A platform connecting businesses with freelance professionals in areas including design and software development.

### PeoplePerHour

https://www.peopleperhour.com/

A freelance platform for finding projects and offering professional services.

## Designer Portfolio & Discovery Platforms

### Dribbble

https://dribbble.com/

A design community where designers can showcase their work and attract potential clients.

### Behance

https://www.behance.net/

A creative portfolio platform used by designers, developers, agencies, and creative professionals.

### LinkedIn

https://www.linkedin.com/

Useful for building a professional presence, networking with founders, and finding freelance opportunities.

## Framer & Webflow Opportunities

### Framer

https://www.framer.com/

Useful for designers specializing in Framer websites and templates.

### Webflow

https://webflow.com/

A major platform and ecosystem for Webflow designers and developers.

## How to Get More Clients

* Build a focused portfolio instead of showing everything you have created.
* Create case studies explaining your design decisions and results.
* Specialize in a specific technology or service.
* Clearly explain who you help.
* Make it easy for potential clients to contact you.
* Share useful design and development content.
* Reach out to relevant founders and businesses.
* Keep your portfolio and profiles updated.
* Ask previous clients for testimonials.
* Respond quickly to relevant project opportunities.
